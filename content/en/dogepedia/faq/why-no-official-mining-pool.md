People sometimes ask for a **Dogecoin.com mining pool** — a pool “owned by the website” or the Foundation that pays only in DOGE and undercuts commercial fees.

## Short answer

**dogecoin.com is an informational website**, not a mining operator. There is **no official Dogecoin.com pool** run as part of this site. Mining and pool operation are separate, competitive businesses (and technical infrastructure) in the ecosystem.

## Why that matters

| Concern | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| **Decentralisation** | One “official” pool would *concentrate* hash rate under a single brand — the opposite of spreading power |
| **Ops & liability** | Pools need 24/7 infrastructure, payouts, security, and legal/tax handling; that is not the website’s role |
| **AuxPoW** | Dogecoin is merge-mined with Litecoin; many pools mine both — commercial choices, not Foundation policy |
| **Core vs products** | [Dogecoin Core](https://github.com/dogecoin/dogecoin) defines the protocol; pools are third-party services on top |

## What *does* exist

- Learn the basics: [What is a miner?](/dogepedia/articles/what-is-a-miner) · [What is a mining pool?](/dogepedia/articles/what-is-a-mining-pool)  
- Mine yourself: [Mining Dogecoin how-to](/dogepedia/how-tos/mining-dogecoin)  
- Foundation priorities live on the [Trailmap](https://foundation.dogecoin.com/trailmap/) — not a mandatory pool product  

Community members are free to start pools, compare fees, and vote with their hashrate. **Do Only Good Everyday** — and pick pools you trust after your own research.
