New to Dogecoin? This short path takes you from zero to sending your first tip — safely.

> **Do Only Good Everyday.** Never share seed phrases or private keys. Nobody from the Dogecoin Foundation, Core developers, or “support” will ever DM you asking for them.

## Step 1 — Learn the basics (5 minutes)

- [What is Dogecoin?](/dogepedia/articles/what-is-dogecoin)
- [How does Dogecoin work?](/dogepedia/articles/how-does-dogecoin-work)
- [What is a wallet, really?](/dogepedia/articles/what-is-a-wallet)

You do not need to mine or run a node to use Dogecoin as money.

## Step 2 — Choose a wallet

A **wallet** holds the keys that control your coins. Prefer **self-custody** (you control the keys) for savings; exchanges can be fine for buying, then withdraw.

1. Open the [wallets directory](/wallets) on dogecoin.com
2. Pick a wallet that fits how you will use DOGE (mobile, desktop, hardware)
3. Read the tags (open source, self-custodial, platforms) and **download only from official links**

More detail: [How do I get a wallet?](/dogepedia/articles/how-do-i-get-a-wallet)

## Step 3 — Back up before you fund it

Before you send any meaningful amount:

1. Write down the seed phrase (or backup `wallet.dat` for Dogecoin Core) **offline**
2. Store it somewhere safe — not in screenshots, email, or cloud notes
3. Test recovery on a second device if you can (with a tiny amount first)

Guide: [How do I backup my wallet?](/dogepedia/articles/how-to-backup-a-wallet) · [Recover a lost wallet](/dogepedia/articles/recover-a-lost-wallet)

## Step 4 — Get some Dogecoin

Common paths:

- Buy on an exchange, then **withdraw to your wallet address**
- Receive a tip from a friend or community bot
- Earn it (work, merchants, Folding@Home, and more)

Guide: [How can I get Dogecoin?](/dogepedia/articles/get-dogecoin)

Always double-check the **first and last characters** of your receive address before you share it.

## Step 5 — Send and receive

1. Get a receive address from your wallet
2. Share it with the sender (or paste it into an exchange withdrawal)
3. Wait for network confirmations — Dogecoin is fast and fees are usually low
4. To send: paste the destination address carefully, choose an amount, confirm

Guide: [How do I send and receive Dogecoin?](/dogepedia/articles/send-and-receive-dogecoin)

## Safety checklist

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Download wallets from official sources | Install random “claim airdrop” apps |
| Back up seeds offline | Type your seed into websites |
| Start with a small test send | Send your life savings on the first try |
| Verify addresses character-by-character | Trust unsolicited DMs offering “support” |
